TEAM STAT TRACKER - offline app (PWA)
=====================================

This folder IS the app. To put it on a phone or tablet you publish the folder
to any free web host once, then "install" it from the browser. After the first
visit it works with no internet at all.

1) PUBLISH THE FOLDER (pick one, all free, needs HTTPS)
   - Netlify Drop: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder onto the page.
   - Cloudflare Pages / GitHub Pages: upload the folder contents as a static site.
   Keep the folder contents together (index.html, sw.js, assets/, icons/ ...).
   Opening index.html straight from your files will NOT install or work offline;
   the offline part needs the https:// address.

2) INSTALL ON THE DEVICE (open the address once while online)
   - Android (Chrome): menu (three dots) > "Install app" / "Add to Home screen".
   - iPhone / iPad (Safari): Share button > "Add to Home Screen".
   Then open it from the new home-screen icon. Wait a couple of seconds the first
   time; the app is saved on the device. After that, airplane mode is fine.

3) GOOD TO KNOW
   - Your teams, players and games are stored on that device only. Phone and tablet
     do not sync with each other.
   - iPhone/iPad: the home-screen app and the Safari tab keep SEPARATE data.
     Install first, then enter your real data in the home-screen app.
   - Clearing the browser's website data / uninstalling the app erases the data.
     Use "Finish & save game" and keep the Excel box score as your backup.
   - To update: upload the new folder to the same address. Open the app online and
     close/reopen it once; the new version is picked up automatically.
   - Needs a reasonably recent phone: Android Chrome 111+ or iOS/iPadOS 16.4+.
